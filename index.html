About

I build Rails applications where a language model does one specific job — not a chatbot bolted on the side, but a model earning its place in a product that would still make sense without it.

I studied computer science in Manila, then spent seven years in a Tokyo restaurant — much of it as the expeditor, the person between the kitchen and the floor making sure the right thing goes out at the right time — before coming back to code through Le Wagon. Most of what I do now is back end: PostgreSQL and PostGIS, external APIs, the parts that have to be right before anything on screen matters.
